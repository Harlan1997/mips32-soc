# Dhrystone Source Provenance

The files `dhry.h`, `dhry_1.c`, and `dhry_2.c` are the C version 2.1
distribution by Reinhold P. Weicker, obtained from the Netlib benchmark
archive at <https://www.netlib.org/benchmark/dhry-c>.

`dhrystone_port.c`, `dhrystone_port.h`, and the build integration are
SoC-specific additions. The original source headers and the accompanying
`README_C`, `RATIONALE`, and `VARIATIONS` files are retained for provenance.
